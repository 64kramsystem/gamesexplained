# Orientation

The [DDT disk from Planet Emulation](https://www.planetemu.net/en/rom/commodore-c64-games-arcade-d64/dan-dare-3-the-escape-1990-virgin-mastertronic-cr-ddt-a) is a 174,848-byte, 35-track D64 sector image. Its opening bytes are program sector bytes, and the kit's D64 reader finds a valid 683-sector directory and allocation map. The sole file is `DAN DARE 3+7/DDT`, a 38,653-byte PRG including its $0801 load address. The source image and snapshots are in ignored `work/`.

Boot in x64sc with the disk on drive 8 and autostart directory entry 1. Wait for the black DDT scroller and press fire on joystick port 1. Wait for the trainer prompts and answer N to all seven. They cover rifle shots, energy, jet fuel, money, lives, smart bombs and bouncing bombs. Wait for the Dan Dare 3 title and its keyboard legend. Use the default keys. Press 1 on the title menu, or fire with Space when the selected control mode accepts it. Wait until Dan and the terminal are visible above the HUD. `work/title.vsf` holds the title and `work/cell-start.vsf` holds the settled opening cell. The listing uses the latter. Captures are [title](reference/title.png) and [opening cell](reference/cell-start.png).

The PRG runs through a crack intro and trainer before the authored game title. The single packed PRG supplies the title and the opening gameplay image. The title-to-play transition rebuilds the VIC display and expands a room map from resident data. The title's copy source at $8921 and destination at $C000 agree for the opening bytes of its character data. Both title and play snapshots are local because character and bitmap pages change between them. The disk has no additional file or stray allocated sectors, and the traced room setup decompresses resident data. A breakpoint at $01E4 and a single instruction step caught the final RTI restoring PC $0600. The JMP there enters setup at $9FAD. A second capture from a stopping execution checkpoint on $0600 reloads at that address and is kept as `work/entry.vsf`. The alternate-release comparison remains open.

During play the processor port read $35 on outputs configured by $2F. The KERNAL vectors at $0314 are unused by this IRQ path. A stopping store checkpoint on $FFFE caught the engine cycling through $25C8, $236D and $26CF. The paired low and high stores at $2727/$272D or $243D/$2443 complete the vector update. The hardware NMI vector read $31D8.

On the restored worktree, disk autostart stalled at LOADING. Extracting the sole PRG with the kit disk reader, hard-resetting VICE, injecting the PRG at its load address with `vice.inject`, and typing `SYS2049` reached the DDT intro. After the trainer, menu option 1 starts the game. Input during the controls legend can first return to the menu. The recovered disk has the same SHA-256 as `media.json`.

VICE is the installed v3.14.0 macOS arm64 headless release. It ran on the assigned port 17416 with the dummy sound device. The emulator check passed 55 checks. It failed checkpoint persistence across a snapshot load, host-key delivery, and a remote-monitor timeout during the quirk probe. For the live checks, arm checkpoints after restoring a snapshot and use matrix keys. regenerator2000 answered on the assigned port 17418.

The restored worktree uses VICE port 23072 and regenerator2000 port 23074. The headless VICE log identifies the dummy audio device. The primary listing uses `work/cell-start.vsf` with `work/entry.vsf` as its loaded-data comparison. The separate local `work/handover-overlay/` listing uses `work/entry.vsf` for the startup code and records that are replaced on the way to play.
