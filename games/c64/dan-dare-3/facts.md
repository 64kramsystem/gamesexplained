## The listing's error rate

The earlier sample, seed 1990, used gpt-6.2-sol and found seven wrong comments among 60. All seven were corrected. The retained metadata records the seed and count. Its draw and verdicts are absent from the restored worktree.

The expanded play listing had 1,265 comments when a separate gpt-6.2-sol checker reviewed the 60 drawn by seed 191136. Seven had wrong details. The resulting estimate is 11.7% with a 95% Wilson interval of 5.8% to 22.2%. The checker may have missed errors. The seven corrections cover the horizontal camera origin, the raster row selection list, a sprite list link, direct room overrides, destination glyphs, map-marker coordinates and energy gauge pixels. The draw and verdicts are in `work/reports/sample-191136/`.
