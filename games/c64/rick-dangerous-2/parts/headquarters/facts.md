# Level load

The streamed disk depacker writes $8A00–$BFFF and $D800–$EFFF. The second span begins under the I/O registers, which are banked out during the corresponding stores. The loader returns at $7CF4.

The overlay carries a planar pattern at $8EA4–$8FDE. It consists of 63 five-byte records. Combining the corresponding bits of the first four bytes gives an eight-position pattern of four-bit indices. The first three bytes OR to $FF in each record, and the fifth byte is zero. The decoded strip has repeated bands, a checker pattern, and diagonals. The patterns’ original purpose and palette are open. The room redraw also reads part of this same data as metatile indices.

The bytes at $BA8B–$BBEE match the Mines overlay exactly. They follow the final indexed Headquarters object template. The retained scene at $AAD5 is referenced by the entry script's opcode thirteen. Its tested handler skips each three-byte command without following the pointer.

The final room directory entry at $A438 gives a map base of $8E40 and a right-scroll limit byte of $10. In a directed instruction trace, the column expander ran with each scroll origin from zero through $13 and the right-edge offset of 40 used by the scroll routines. The 40 distinct reads at $5056 covered $8E90–$8EB7. That bound describes scrolling from within the room's regular camera range.

The incoming exit at $A798 can set a higher origin. Its comparison column is $17 and its destination column byte is $1F. The exit handler subtracts the player's screen-relative offset and the room initializer adds two to the stored entry column. In two directed CPU traces at $1A69, source camera origins 14 and 22 transferred to origins 22 and 30 respectively. VICE stopped at $1B60 after the initial 40-strip redraw. Each run executed $5056 320 times. The first watch covered $8EB8–$8EBF and counted 16 reads. The second covered $8EB8–$8ECF and counted 80 reads. The corresponding upper-range watches counted zero reads. The three eight-byte groups at $8EB8–$8ECF serve as metatile indices when that redraw extends the lookahead buffer. The incoming transfer subtracts a quantity from zero through 31 from the destination column $1F. Even the maximum origin of 31 plus the redraw’s last offset of 39 selects metatile column 17, ending at $8ECF.

A store provenance trace at $874B and $879F followed the 320-byte span from $8E9F through $8FDE, which spans 64 candidate five-byte rows. The depacker wrote 93 literal bytes, 218 bytes copied within the region and nine bytes copied from other destinations. Each copy source had already been written on that load. The first of those 64 candidate rows fails the full three-mask coverage test. The other 63 rows match it.

A read trace of the three Headquarters story streams at $A8E8, $AB26 and $ABE5 also found zero CPU data reads in $8ED0–$8FDC. The simulation supplied raster and timer values at the waits and let each script return through the game’s interpreter.

The other locally available disk has an identical packed fifth level file. The depacker trace consumed the full file and reproduced every byte in the Headquarters loader-return snapshot. A search for contiguous little-endian pointers to $8ED0 and the five-byte record boundaries in resident code and overlay directories yielded no candidates. A ranged load watch during two seconds of warped execution after opcode thirteen in the entry story also counted zero reads at $8ED0–$8FDC. Those checks leave the original use of the planar data open.
