# Level load

The streamed disk depacker writes $8A00–$BFFF and $D800–$EFFF. The second span begins under the I/O registers, which are banked out during the corresponding stores. The loader returns at $7CF4. The use and format of each subrange remain open.

The remaining bare span is $8EB8–$8FDC, 293 bytes. The selected engine listing's direct and indexed reference search found no instruction naming $8EB8. A ranged CPU load watch during two seconds of warped execution after the opcode-thirteen stop in the entry story counted zero hits. The VIC's selected bank begins at $C000, so the chip cannot read this span directly in that state. The evidence covers this scene and mapping. The span remains counted because its depacker writes were traced and its consumer is unresolved.

The bytes at $BA8B–$BBEE match the Mines overlay exactly. They follow the final indexed Headquarters object template. The retained scene at $AAD5 is referenced by the entry script's opcode thirteen. Its tested handler skips each three-byte command without following the pointer.
