# Features

| Status | Feature and evidence |
|---|---|
| live | The boot offers English, German and Italian. The English route reaches the globe selector. |
| live | Control port 2 moves the pointer. The left globe selects Fire and the right globe selects Ice. The centre hotspot opens the high scores. |
| live | Each of the two tested globes offers Easy, Medium and Hard on keys 1, 2 and 3. Fire advances the world picture and Space starts play from the quest prompt. |
| live | Fire starts next to a sign on rocky ground. Ice starts on snow with a snowfall effect. Both show a score, lives, a centre panel and an energy bar. |
| open | Movement, jumping, crouching, fall damage, hazards, projectile limits and level scrolling. |
| open | The manual describes weapon pickups and upgrades, food, treasure, runes, spells, teleporters and a trader. The exact set and controls on C64 need tracing. |
| open | Castle unlock after the two worlds, the king and the winning sequence. |
| open | Difficulty effects, scoring, death, retries, score entry and music selection. |

The screenshots in `reference/` were captured in the Legend disk release. The manual is shared across versions and its platform-specific controls need separate C64 evidence.
