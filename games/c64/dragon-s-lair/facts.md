# Dragon's Lair — technical facts

## Loader and memory

- The resident disk loader at `$0334` adds `$40` to the selector and patches the second byte of a two-byte name beginning with `L`. It switches the processor port to `$37` for KERNAL I/O and uses device 8, secondary address 1. Instruction stops at `$FFD5` recorded requests for `LA` through `LH` when the resident dispatcher at `$0CD1` received indices 1 through 8. The dragon code at `$1E0F` makes the additional `LI` request with selector 9.
- The staged scene number at `$8010` is compared with `$26` at `$0DBB`. A mismatch selects the embedded first scene at RAM `$D010`. When it matches, the file contents are staged at `$8010`. The copy loop at `$0DE8` reads through `$2A/$2B` with I/O banked away, restores I/O, and writes to `$1E00` through `$2C/$2D`. It includes the eleven bytes `END OF CODE`, compared against the table at `$1C40`. The shared caller at `$0CA2` or `$0CAC` begins execution at `$1E01`, one byte after the identifier.
- The graphics stream begins on the page after the marker. The decoder at `$0E37` takes literals unless the byte equals the selector-specific value from `$1CBA`. An escape consumes the repeated byte and a count. It clips the output against the exclusive limit `$7FFF`, or `$9FFF` for scene 8. A count of zero can perform 256 writes with the eight-bit countdown. The measured stream boundaries are in orientation.md.
- The second descent scans the embedded record again at `$1E16`. The stop at `$1E58` showed input `$E200` and output `$4000` in the two zero-page pointers. Stepping `LDA ($2A),Y` at `$0E3C` read `$54`, the escape byte. After the transformation loop at `$1E5D`, a stop at `$1E72` showed an exact match to first-descent entry RAM across `$4000–$7DFF`. There were 140 differences in the transformed `$7E00–$7FFF` pages. The first descent owns the common image and the second owns the transformed pages.
- At `$1E12` in the dragon scene, the 2030 bytes at `$9A00–$A1ED` match the `LI` file exactly. The retained `LH` bytes at `$A1EE–$BF0F` are sometimes read as raw data as well as staging for decompression. For example, `$22A9` copies 65 bytes from `$A9F9` to the sprite area at `$6800`, and `$21E7` takes the low nibbles of five bytes at `$B002–$B006`.

- Decoding the `LH` stream from `$9400` to the end of disk data with escape byte `$2D` emits 23,154 bytes. Its first 23,040 bytes match the post-`LI` snapshot through `$99FF`. The compressed token starting at `$BE9D` emits bytes at `$99FF–$9A01`, straddling the start of the replacement data from `LI`. The ledger keeps that token and the rest of the packed tail, along with the raw sprite and palette sources.

- The first dragon palette transfer starts at `$8216`, ends at `$8600` inclusive, and writes to `$6000–$63EA`. Stepping the indexed load at `$2302` with Y=0 returned `$22`. At the next transfer setup the source and destination pointers were `$8600` and `$63EA`, and the full destination matched the source.
- The dragon's opening bitmap update patches `$2B13` to read from `$9255` with offsets `$7F` down to zero for each of eleven strips. The first destination is `$4040`. The paired colour update patches `$2ABD` and `$2AC3` to read from `$7D65` and `$7C65`. Starting the existing calls at `$20A8` and `$20D9` with their caller's arguments produced 144-byte strips from `$87EB` and 56-byte strips from `$8600` respectively.
- The recurring dragon bitmap patch at `$2AEB` selects between `$8FB5` and `$9105` using `$0414`. Stepping the patched instruction at `$2B13` for both values read the first strip with Y=`$37` and X=5. Both copies start at destination `$4B68` and advance a full bitmap row between strips.
- The second descent rebuilds three character matrices from the same embedded stream. The first source instruction at `$29B5` read escape byte `$54` from `$E200`, and the call to the colour routine at `$2A29` had source `$E796`, output `$4C00` and selected screen page `$40`. The raster routine at `$2905` mirrors the eight sprite pointers from the first matrix into the other two. Stepping the first load and both stores confirmed the pointer at offset 7 reached both destinations. The collision code at `$2B10` compares corresponding cells in all three matrices. In the captured opening state, stepping that instruction read character 2 using pointers `$411D`, `$451D` and `$491D` for the three comparisons.

- The dragon's bitmap selectors 1, 2 and 5 read eleven 128-byte strips from `$97E5`, `$9D75` and `$A307` respectively. For each controlled selector, stepping the patched load at `$2B13` confirmed the first source byte, and the destination at `$4CC0` matched the last strip. Selector 2 reads from both LI and the next `$107` retained LH bytes. Selector 5 reads entirely from retained LH bytes. Stepping `$2ABD` and `$2AC3` also confirmed the two colour sources for each selector.

- The smaller dragon patches selected by Y=1, 2 and 3 at `$2E56` use bitmap sources `$7B91`, `$7ABE` and `$79EB`. Stepping the patched loads at `$2B13`, `$2ABD` and `$2AC3` checked the bitmap, colour RAM and screen matrix sources. The bitmap copies four strips of forty bytes. The palette operands select four rows of five cells.

- The retained 518-byte block at `$37FA–$39FF` decodes to the 692 ending-matrix cells at `$4CE1–$4F94` in the first-descent entry snapshot. Each nonzero source byte is a literal. Zero introduces the count of blank cells. The match identifies the contents of the retained block. Its original reader remains open.
- The title snapshot contains a page-copy stub at `$C070–$C089`. A store watchpoint at `$C071` during relocation stopped at `$9F0D` with Y equal to `$71` and the destination matching `$6071`. The title snapshot therefore holds the stub, while the resident snapshot holds the copied sound code at that address.
- The 16 bytes under I/O at `$D000–$D00F` match the second-descent sprite setup at `$2E2E–$2E3D`, including the load and store that enable sprite 1. In the resident snapshot they sit immediately before the embedded first-descent record.

The numbered-load opcode census across all eleven part images found the resident call at `$0CD3` and the dragon supplement call at `$1E0F`. The resident `$037F` reaches KERNAL LOAD at `$FFD5`. The resident scene index starts at zero and advances on successful returns from scenes 0–7. The dragon runs as scene 8, requests `LI` with a literal selector of 9, and exits to the resident score path at `$0CD9`. Selector 10 would form `LJ`. That file's single byte `$00` fails the comparison with 10 at `$0DBB–$0DC2` and takes the first-scene fallback in a controlled probe.

## Input and timing

- The castle picture exits through the relocation at `$9F00` into the text prologue at `$4401`. In a controlled run from the title snapshot, the first scroll-row load at `$45A0` read `$44` from `$4727`, the rightmost byte of the first row. The full glyph page at `$2800` matched its source at `$5000`. The scroll copies twenty rows upward, inserts the next row at `$06F8` and uses a single high-bit source byte to request a blank row.
- The first descent tests the saved active-low fire bit at `$1FB2` when deciding whether to start a jump. Stepping the three instructions with a released sample of `$FF` reached the return at `$1FB8`. A sample of `$EF` reached the setup at `$1FB9`.

- The initial castle picture takes fire on port 1. From the title snapshot, stepping the read at `$C053` returned `$EF` with port 1 fire and `$FF` with port 2 fire. The next two instructions advanced to `$C05A` with port 1 held and returned to `$C053` with port 2 held.
- The inter-scene prompt at `$0CF2` takes fire on port 2. Stepping its read and branch with port 1 pressed returned to `$0CF2` and with port 2 pressed advanced to `$0CF9`. The live port samples were `$7F` and `$6F` respectively. During gameplay the common routine at `$1592` writes `$FF` to CIA1 port A, latches the input at `$28` and restores `$7F`. Several rooms also read the port directly and invert the input before comparing exact masks.
- CIA2 timer A provides polling delays at `$15A5` and a separate NMI-counter wait at `$1625`. The latter compares `$156F` before and after the NMI handler at `$1566` increments it. The resident raster handler at `$1647` acknowledges VIC raster bit 0 and dispatches through `$42/$43`. The hallway changes these pointers between raster bands so hardware sprites 5–7 can represent different body pieces in a single frame.

- The dragon raster dispatcher at `$28EF` patches the jump operand at `$2915/$2916` from the split address table at `$3054/$3059`. Five consecutive steps of the jump reached `$239F`, `$23F4`, `$24B5`, `$24FA` and `$2522`. The line table at `$305E` schedules those phases at `$00`, `$33`, `$80`, `$DA` and `$F8`. The later phases reuse sprite 7 by saving and restoring its position and frame pointer.

- The high-score initials editor begins polling port 2 at `$145D`. From the saved resident snapshot, stepping the polling instructions with one control held at a time reached `$1491` for up, `$149E` for down, `$14AB` for left, `$14B5` for right and `$14D4` for fire. With the stick released it reached the colour-increment path at `$1485`. The up and down handlers wrap within the `$60–$7A` screen-code range.

## Graphics and text

- The Skull Hallway raster chain includes `$2C69` after `$2C27`. Stepping each dispatch store confirmed the six-handler cycle from `$2B2E` back to itself. The Weapons Room and Tentacle Room each use their own six-handler chain for the same three sprite slots, starting at `$2C07` and `$2B34` respectively.
- The Weapons Room sprite builder selects a packed source from `$2ECC` and assembles the image at `$6A40`. With index 5, the trace read a leading-zero count of 45 at `$2FBE` and copied the remaining 19 bytes. The same controlled call worked in the copy retained in the Ramps image. With index 3, the Weapons Room, Skull Hallway and Tentacle Room copies all produced 60 zero bytes and four literals. The final literal doubles as the first byte of the next packed record.
- The Weapons Room cell animation selector at `$2DFF` resolved record 0 to screen address `$4338`. Stepping the timed update through `$2DBB` wrote character `$F9` there and colour 1 at `$DB38`. This was an isolated call using the room snapshot.
- In the first descent the overlay selector at `$2A14` points to `$2B7D`. The first indexed load at `$2A39` read zero at `$2B8B`, column 14 of the first row. The failure display selects the `$4C00` matrix and glyph base `$7000` through `$D018=$3C`.
- A controlled Phantom Knight board lookup for target column 3 and row 4 reached `$27B4` with X=43. Stepping the load read type 1 from `$2E29`, the entry at `$2DFE + 43`.
- In Phantom Knight, stepping `$2933` for the four direction values wrote `$C0`, `$A8`, `$A8` and `$C0` in order to the staged sprite-pointer slot at `$2DD1`.
- The dragon short bitmap patch at `$2E0E` selects `$5F40` or `$5430` from the words at `$3132`. Stepping the first patched read and comparing both destination rows confirmed that each copy draws two 43-byte strips starting at `$4D7B`.

- The castle interstitial uses screen page `$0400` and glyphs from `$0800` in VIC bank zero. Stepping the `STA $D018` at `$10A8` left `$D018=$13` and `$DD00=$C7`. Eight-byte glyphs at `$0B00–$0BFF` include the letters selected by the high-score heading's `AND #$1F` and `ADC #$60` mapping. Two four-cell patterns at `$1B24` and `$1B28` supply the brick rows.

- Attract uses the character screen at `$0400`. A store watchpoint there stopped with PC `$15F1` just after the `STA ($2A),Y` in the shared display-clear loop. Sprite pointers at `$07F8–$07FF` are also written by `$125F` as part of the interstitial setup.
- The first descent, Skull Hallway, Burning Ropes, Weapons Room, Ramps and Tentacle Room play captures show the `$4000` VIC bank in character mode with `$D018=$0F`, screen at `$4000` and glyphs from `$7800`. The board capture sets `$D018=$F1` in the same bank and uses bitmap mode, with bitmap at `$4000` and matrix at `$7C00`. The dragon capture uses `$D018=$81`, bitmap at `$4000` and matrix at `$6000`.
- The ten eight-byte score glyphs at `$0A60–$0AAF` render as digits 0 through 9. The copier at `$1880` with A equal to `$78` installs them at `$7D80`, corresponding to glyphs `$B0–$B9` of the scene character set. The game also holds PETSCII title and credit strings beginning at `$19E4`.
- The resident sprite helper at `$16AD` compares the requested orientation in `$03EF` against the 64-byte record’s final byte. With pointer `$60` and the orientation changed, stepping the first store at `$16FB` wrote the reversed third byte of the first row. All 21 rows were reversed horizontally on return, and the final byte held the requested orientation.
- The resident failure entry at `$0ECD` clears all three BCD score bytes before drawing the castle. Stepping `$0EDE` cleared the last byte of the test score. The display routine decrements `$27` and branches to the failure message at zero.
- The castle retry sequence selects additional sprite frames through the tables at `$1BFA`, `$1C0C` and `$1C1A`. Stepping the first load and store in each installer wrote `$EF` to `$07FF`, `$FB` to `$07FB` and `$FF` to `$07FB` respectively. The tables address the frame ranges `$3A00–$3BFF`, `$3E00–$3EFF` and `$3F00–$3FFF` in VIC bank zero.
- The shared pose records begin at `$3200`. The Skull Hallway instructions at `$1E70–$1E75` copy 90 bytes to `$44–$9D` with X descending. At the first read, stepping the load with X=`$59` returned `$D0`. The selectors at `$2ABA` in that scene, `$2B34` in Weapons Room and `$2A61` in Tentacle Room use a stride of 90. The hallway raster handlers starting at `$2B2E` read sprite positions, colours, pointers and expansion masks from these zero-page records.

- Burning Ropes dispatches its background repaint at `$2A72` through the sixteen words at `$2F29`. Stepping the jump with a controlled selector reached `$2AEC` for stage 0, `$2A7C` for stage 14 and `$2A75` for the final table slot. The decoded selector writes range from 0 through 14. A later entry in the cascade begins with fewer screen patches to redraw.
- Weapons Room dispatches a matrix clear at `$28CA` through the four words at `$292C`. Stepping the jump with A from 0 through 3 at `$28BE` yielded `$28ED`, `$28E0`, `$28DD` and `$28CD`. The branch at `$28CD` clears three rows of four cells before falling through to the shorter clears.

The ninth decoded from `$4B50–$4BDA` into `$42F8–$43E6`. The bytes between records remain in the ledger.

- The second descent disk tail at `$9195–$91FF` matches the Weapons Room overlay table at `$309C–$3106`. The overlay's 18-byte records comprise eight coordinate bytes, four colours, four frame pointers and two expansion masks. With index 4 at `$2B56`, stepping `$2B7A` read the first colour byte from `$309C`. The copy on the second descent disk remains counted. Its role in that load is open.

- The Burning Ropes initializer copies four pages from `$7000` to `$4000`. Stepping the indexed load at `$2C0E` read the first character, and at `$2C1C` the complete destination matched the template.
- The Burning Ropes raster routine selects the second matrix and moves sprites behind the characters before the colour bands. ## The listing's error rate

Seed 65022026 drew 70 of 1,671 comments. An independent gpt-6.2-sol checker found five wrong details and left one sprite-bank usage claim unverifiable. The estimated error rate was 7.1% with a 95% Wilson interval of 3.1%–15.7%. The estimate weighted by stratum was 6.6%. The checker may have missed errors. The five wrong comments were corrected, including the shared SID repeat wording across 86 pattern comments. The other corrections distinguish stack save from restore in Burning Ropes, give the first-descent collision rejection conditions, and identify the Weapons Room strip clear. The tentative sprite-bank usage wording was narrowed to the 16 bitmap records and their VIC pointer range.

## Open

The documented Giddy Goons set piece still has no confirmed scene in this disk edition. The disk directory, scene headers, loader requests and scene captures account for the scenes named in orientation.md. The original use of some retained records, including the first-descent ending-matrix tail at resident `$37FA`, is open.
