The analysis covers the resident engine, seven island loads and the
ending. The exact exported ledgers and the rebuilt listings determine
coverage. Each part owns its symbol map and listing. The top-level
coverage command aggregates them.

- The site, reader review and retrospective are outside this handoff.
- Keep the tier at Bronze until the required proven-model check.
  The initial comment sample found five wrong details in 95 draws.
  All five were corrected. Sampled accuracy and a full listing audit
  are separate requirements.
- Later island and ending snapshots were reached using the game's
  loader and saved-state selectors. Normal progression, boss
  conditions, the ordered diamond route and the alternate ending
  condition are open in the feature checklist.
- Sources, breakpoint scripts, rendered glyph sheets, sample verdicts
  and snapshots remain local under `work/`. The resident and ending
  sound pairing report is in `work/sound-pair-report.txt`.
