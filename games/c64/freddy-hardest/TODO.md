resource loads. This disk has none of those named resources. Their

data cannot be verified against this disk alone. The matching byte run

play. They cannot establish absence from later states. Remaining
tracked gaps include branch fragments and tables in the action and
sound paths.

`facts.md` covers shared findings and links the two maps. The original disk
image, extracted PRGs, snapshots and emulator experiments stay local.

reader. Surface and base captures use short orientation inputs. Step 65
requires a portable recording for a replay-dependent claim. This handoff
makes no such claim.
