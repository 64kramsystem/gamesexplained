# Blood Money features

Sources consulted on 19 November 2026 include the Accept disk's title and options screens, the [C64 review at Lemon64](https://www.lemon64.com/review/blood-money/348), and the [C64 catalog at Games Database](https://www.gamesdatabase.org/game/commodore-64/blood-money).

| Feature | Status | Evidence or question |
|---|---|---|
| Animated title and credits | live | Title screen in the Accept disk. |
| Title theme and in-game sound | open | The review describes music on the title. Identify the playback entry points and tune selections. |
| One- and two-player modes | open | The C64 review describes both. The option text identifies 1 and 2 as the player-count keys. Simultaneous play and controls for the second player are open. |
| Options for faster text and viewing high scores | open | Documented by the C64 review. The options text at $187E identifies 4 as the text pacing switch. Score persistence is open. |
| Four missions with side-view scrolling | live | Four level loads reached play. The loader selects files with the digit set at $C8BF. Direction bytes at $8004 are consumed at $CC6A and $9E28. |
| Shooting enemies releases money | open | The review reports that larger enemies can drop more valuable coins. |
| Dropped money falls out of reach | open | Documented by the C64 review. Resolve collection and expiry. |
| Shops sell upgrades | open | Listed by Games Database. Identify items, costs and changes to the vehicle. |
| Contact with walls or enemies drains energy | open | C64 review and catalog. Determine which entities take energy and when lives are lost. |
| Some enemies take money | open | C64 catalog. Find their behavior and whether it replaces contact damage. |
