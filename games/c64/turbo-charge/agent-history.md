# Turbo Charge — agent history

The Nostalgia disk build reached the trainer with matrix Space, then
the title and first drive with joystick port 2 fire. Dummy audio was
selected in the assigned launcher. The VICE capability report and raw
traces are in ignored work files.

A full-memory monitor reply once ended partway through the requested
store map even though the stage had loaded. Querying 4 KB slices
recovered the recorded write ranges. The monitor also dropped the
emulator in a restore-and-resume sequence. Restarting the same headless
build and reattaching disks allowed the capture work to continue.

Early debugger shortcuts loaded all stages from round 1. Comparing
the music pages and loader writes exposed an inherited tune dependency
for even stages. The final even-stage snapshots were captured from
their previous odd stage. The working directory retains the first
captures and the corrected pairs for comparison.

The listing and coverage manifest are organized by loads. Stage 1
holds the resident driving engine, later driving parts own their
observed replacements and each distinct interstitial owns its picture
planes. The game-wide technical notes and the per-part facts stay with
the analysis for handoff.
