# Combat

- The introductory text accepts port-2 fire through $EC4C and $EC62. Independent entry produced a mountain backdrop and two fighters. It omitted the training results and therefore does not establish the observed default score and hit values for ordinary play.
- The routine at $1E9E decodes four background streams identified by instruction tracing. Its first stream begins at $D000 and is read with the chips banked out. All four decode to the same display ranges.
- The transition at $C300 restores the ROM mapping and requests HIGH. Its call at $C326 reached the KERNAL LOAD entry, and the new program ran from $C200.
- Instruction tracing of $1CB4 recorded zero stores to $2000–$3FE0 and $5180–$5E73. Combat calls it before $1E9E decodes the selected scene. The introduction first copies its own glyphs from RAM under I/O to $2000–$22FF at $EDB2–$EDC1. The HUD setup at $1F52 reads 41 authored colour bytes at $4C40–$4C68 and uses the middle byte in both of its 21-cell strips.
- The interlude uses the state byte at $CD4D to select one of seven lists of message IDs through $EE4A. Each list requests two messages. The nine message streams use column and row prefixes, with $FE for a new positioned row and $FF for end. The renderer adds the selected 40-cell row offset from $EE18 to the column.
- In 24 VICE frames the raster IRQ at $1D35 ran 120 times. The phase entries at $1D81, $1DB0, $1DC8 and $1E8E ran 24 times each. The phase at $1E57 polls for raster $F1 and its first instruction was fetched 330 times. The score painter at $CA00 and bar service at $0F0D each ran 24 times.
- Combat formats the current score as five PETSCII digits by summing the four nibble contributions. A trace of $CA96 produced 00000, 09999 and 65535 for the inputs 0, 9999 and 65535. Each call fetched eight pointer bytes and 20 digit bytes.
- The bar painters at $0FB7 and $1002 read authored bitmap cells. CPU tests over their position bounds read $4B00–$4B9F for the first bar and $4BA8–$4C3F for the second.
- The first 82 fighter bitmap records are contiguous and have dimensions that predict the next offset at $8000. Controlled execution of movement script 12 at $157D selected frames 80, 81, 82 and 83 in order. Frame 82 at $C254 declares seven rows of 32 bytes, and the painter reads bytes inside the exit at $C300–$C330. Frame 83 points to $C336, which holds instruction operands $12 and $D0. The painter interprets these as 18 rows of 208 bytes, and the tested render sampled one byte in each oversized row at addresses including $C4D0, $C810 and $D1D0. This measures the painter in a controlled state.
- The single-apprentice opponent routine at $C500 writes a synthetic stick byte at $780C. Tests reached the idle, far-distance, move-specific and fallback sites. It used CIA timer bits for the fallback command lookup at $C774.
- The bitmap painter uses $78E7 to preserve background pairs wherever the source pair is zero, and $79E7 to reverse the four two-bit pixels in each byte. All 256 entries matched their respective bit rules.
- The music driver at $E003 clears SID and player state before loading the initial tracks and divider. Instruction tracing of $E000 reached its track, phrase, pulse and pitch tables. The data contains a separate tune credit for the arrangement of G’D Old Scotch.
