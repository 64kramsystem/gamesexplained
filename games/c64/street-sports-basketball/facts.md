## The listing's error rate

The independent sample used seed 20261120 and drew 80 of 1,998 comments across both parts. The gpt-6.2-sol checker found 15 wrong details and one claim about a prior watch window that it could not verify from the images. All 15 reported errors were corrected, including the related families of colour-row limits and base-pose storage comments. The sample estimates an error rate of 18.8%, with a 95% Wilson interval of 11.7% to 28.7%. Weighting by stratum size gives 17.2%. The checker may have missed other errors. A later full audit can measure and correct details beyond this sample.
